## Source & nature of the data

This dataset is **derived from publicly accessible web pages of The Hong Kong
Polytechnic University (PolyU)**. Each question record carries a `_source_urls`
field pointing to the originating page(s); this serves as attribution to, and a
provenance record for, the source content. The dataset authors do not claim
ownership of the underlying PolyU web content, which remains the property of its
respective rights holders.

The released records (questions, reference answers, gold-block ids, tags) are an
original annotation effort by the dataset authors over that public content.
